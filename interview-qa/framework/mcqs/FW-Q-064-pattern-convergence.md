## Question

15 teams use POM, Screenplay, and raw tests. Platform team should:

## Options

1. Support all three equally forever
2. Delete two teams' tests
3. Pick one sanctioned pattern + migrate incrementally
4. Switch to Selenium

## Correct answer

Pick one sanctioned pattern + migrate incrementally

## Why correct

Shared framework requires convergence — D26 scenario.

## Why the others are wrong

- Option 1: Portability destroyed.
- Option 2: Not practical.
- Option 4: Unrelated migration.
